## Dishes

### Crisps and Jamon

### King oyster mushroom 'scallops'
King oyster mushroom rounds poached in dashi, seared. Served in garlic beurre monte with shallot vinaigrette on the side

### Provoleta
Provoletta round, grilled and served with a muscat/port fruit mince (or relish)

### Beef or Tuna Carpaccio
Pounded slices of steak, topped with capers, shallots, rocket. Drizzled with olive oil, and lemon juice. Toasted focaccia on the side.

### Braised Leeks
Olive oil poached leeks, served with toasted breadcrumbs

### Meatballs au poivre
Meatballs with a pepper cream sauce. Topped with crispy potato straws. Baguette on the side

### Steak
Tomahawk sliced with chimmichurri, mustard, horseradish

### Papas bravas
Roasted potatos with spicy vodka tomato sauce and dill mayo

### Mushroom pintxos
Pan fried whole mushrooms in a wine, garlic, shallot gravy.
Served skewered on baguette rounds.

### Date and olive tapenade
Pan fried dates and green olives. Tossed with parsley and spread on feta/goats cheese atop toasted focaccia batons (or fluffy flatbread?)
- Top with honey?

### Burrata
With torched peaches, strawberries, basil and balsamic glaze.
- Needs some crunch?

### Lemon and olive oil tiramisu
Tiramisu inspired. Lemon curd layer, limoncello soaked focaccia and topped with a drizzle of olive oil

### Chocolate mousse
Classic chocolate mouse. Serve with raspberries.
