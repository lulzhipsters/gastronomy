# Bagels

## Ingredients

### Dough
- Bread flour (100%)
- Water (50%)
- Yeast (0.5%)
- Sugar (3%)

### Bath
- Water (enough to float bagels)
- Barley malt or golden syrup (2-4 tablespoons, until water is dark, about halfway to black tea)
- Baking soda (1 tablespoon)

## Method
1. Mix ingredients. Let sit for 15-30 minutes.
2. Knead. 15-20 minutes by hand.
3. Rise, covered, until doubled in size.
4. Divide to ~130g per bagel.
5. Pre-shape and rest for 10 minutes.
6. Final Shape.
7. Rest in fridge for ~12 hours.
8. Remove from fridge and allow to come up to room temperature.
9. With bath ingredients mixed and simmering, 'boil' each bagel for 1 minute each side.
10. (optional) Roll in toppings.
11. Bake for 15-20 minutes, flipping for last 5 to ensure bottoms brown equally.

## Notes
- Tried using 1% yeast, but even a 14h overnight fridge rest over-proofed. To adjust, I dropped the yeast amount.
- Boiling from room temperature purportedly makes for a better rise. Still testing this.
